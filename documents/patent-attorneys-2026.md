## How to Use This Directory

1. **Verify** each attorney's specialty and experience before outreach — confirm they handle ICU/medical device software patents specifically
2. **Check licensing** at uspto.gov (search "USPTO registered patent attorney")
3. **Ask about micro-entity status** — AlertPulse qualifies; many attorneys are unfamiliar with the process
4. **Request flat fee quotes** — most firms will quote a fixed price for non-provisional drafting vs. hourly
5. **Confirm FHIR experience** — attorney should understand FHIR R4 as a structured output format

## Key Questions to Ask During Consultation

1. Have you filed ICU alarm / physiological monitor patents before?
2. What's your experience with FHIR-based medical software patents?
3. Do you understand EU AI Act Article 11/12 compliance for SaMD?
4. Can you file as a micro-entity on our behalf? (saves ~$1,385 vs. regular filing)
5. What's your flat fee for non-provisional drafting? (vs. hourly)
6. Do you run a prior art search as part of the drafting package?
7. What's the typical timeline from engagement to filing?
8. Will you provide a claim strategy document before drafting?
